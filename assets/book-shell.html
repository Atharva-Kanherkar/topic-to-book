Two · Follow-up laddersWhere it goes next

Chapter twoThe ladder they climb

An interviewer rarely asks one question. They ask one, then follow your answer down. Each rung below is a real follow-up, with what the three grades of answer sound like.

They open withThe opening question, verbatim as reported.

weak
The answer that recites a definition.
solid
The answer that names the trade-off and picks a side.
strong
The answer that names the trade-off, picks a side, and states the condition under which it would pick the other.

ThenThe follow-up that tests whether you meant it.

weak
Retreats from the earlier claim.
strong
Holds it, and quantifies.

ThenThe question that only someone who has operated this can answer.

strong
The failure mode, how it is detected, and what the first mitigation is.

Note Ladder rungs come from reported interview accounts; the grading language is this book's, not any company's.
